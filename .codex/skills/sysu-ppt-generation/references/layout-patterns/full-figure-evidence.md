# full-figure-evidence

- Compatibility: variance 2-7, density 3-7, energy 3-7.
- Use: the evidence itself carries the slide claim.
- Not for: unreadable multi-panel journal figures.
- Sketch: action title; figure occupies 70-85% of usable canvas; short source note near figure.
- Budget: one figure or one coordinated panel group; one highlighted comparison.
- Typography/spacing: axis labels normally at least 18 pt and ticks at least 16 pt after redraw.
- Assets: original plots, microscopy, model visualizations, or result images.
- Anti-patterns: long bullets beneath the figure, remote citation, stretched image.
- QA: labels, units, sample size/statistics, crop disclosure, source, projection readability.
