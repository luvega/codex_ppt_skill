# two-column-teaching

- Compatibility: variance 2-6, density 4-6, energy 2-5.
- Use: concept/example, before/after, question/explanation, or text/visual teaching pair.
- Not for: three or more peer items.
- Sketch: two coordinated columns with a clear reading order and stable gutter.
- Budget: one teaching point per column; one shared action title.
- Typography/spacing: body 18-22 pt when possible; gutter at least the style token.
- Assets: one image or diagram per column only when both are necessary.
- Anti-patterns: equal columns with unrelated content, mirrored card stacks, tiny labels.
- QA: column balance, reading order, gutter, title-to-content relationship.
