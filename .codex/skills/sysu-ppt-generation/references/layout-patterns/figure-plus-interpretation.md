# figure-plus-interpretation

- Compatibility: variance 2-7, density 4-6, energy 3-6.
- Use: a figure requires one short interpretive callout.
- Not for: narrative pages with no evidence.
- Sketch: 60-70% figure and 30-40% interpretation, aligned on one baseline grid.
- Budget: one figure; one conclusion; at most three evidence notes.
- Typography/spacing: interpretation 18-22 pt; citation attached to the figure region.
- Assets: charts, tables-as-images, diagrams, or screenshots with clear provenance.
- Anti-patterns: figure shrunk by a long paragraph, duplicate legend, nested callout cards.
- QA: direct labels, visual hierarchy, citation proximity, balanced region sizes.
