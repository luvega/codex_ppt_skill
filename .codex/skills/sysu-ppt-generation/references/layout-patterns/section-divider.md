# section-divider

- Compatibility: variance 1-7, density 1-3, energy 2-7.
- Use: begin a major argument or teaching chapter.
- Not for: ordinary content transitions.
- Sketch: chapter title anchored to the source grid; optional single image or short orienting line.
- Budget: one title, optional one-sentence bridge, one identity element.
- Typography/spacing: title 28-36 pt where the source permits; preserve large empty regions.
- Assets: source divider layouts, campus imagery, or one meaningful section visual.
- Anti-patterns: agenda repeated as decoration, large chapter numbers without meaning, extra cards.
- QA: title fit, brand fidelity, visual center, clean transition from adjacent slides.
