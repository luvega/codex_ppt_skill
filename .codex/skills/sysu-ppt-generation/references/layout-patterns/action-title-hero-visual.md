# action-title-hero-visual

- Compatibility: variance 3-8, density 2-5, energy 3-8.
- Use: one decisive claim supported by one dominant image, diagram, or number.
- Not for: several equally important results.
- Sketch: action title; 55-70% visual region; one concise interpretation.
- Budget: one claim, one visual, at most two supporting statements.
- Typography/spacing: body normally 18-22 pt; keep the visual larger than the text block.
- Assets: real figure, campus image, model output, or high-resolution diagram.
- Anti-patterns: fake dashboard, stock icon collage, three equal cards around the visual.
- QA: visual relevance, aspect ratio, action-title clarity, citation proximity.
