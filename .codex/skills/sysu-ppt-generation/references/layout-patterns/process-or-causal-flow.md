# process-or-causal-flow

- Compatibility: variance 3-7, density 3-6, energy 3-7.
- Use: workflow, mechanism, chronology, or causal chain.
- Not for: unordered lists.
- Sketch: three to six steps on one clear reading path; optional evidence branch.
- Budget: one verb-led label and one short explanation per step.
- Typography/spacing: step labels at least 18 pt; connectors remain visually secondary.
- Assets: simple native shapes, source icons only when semantically useful.
- Anti-patterns: fake dashboard, ornamental arrows, crossing connectors, all steps in separate cards.
- QA: direction, sequence, connector clarity, endpoint emphasis, no overlaps.
