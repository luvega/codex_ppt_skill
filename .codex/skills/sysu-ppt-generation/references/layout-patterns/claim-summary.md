# claim-summary

- Compatibility: variance 2-7, density 2-5, energy 3-7.
- Use: conclusion, teaching recap, recommendation, or decision page.
- Not for: repeating the agenda or every slide title.
- Sketch: one synthesis statement plus two to four implications/actions.
- Budget: one main claim; no more than four concise supporting items.
- Typography/spacing: main claim visibly dominates; supporting text normally at least 18 pt.
- Assets: optional single summary diagram or restrained numbered sequence.
- Anti-patterns: generic thank-you page, three identical decorative cards, slogan without evidence.
- QA: synthesis quality, hierarchy, actionability, consistency with preceding evidence.
